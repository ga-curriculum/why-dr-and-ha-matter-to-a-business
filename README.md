## About

By the end of this module, students will be able to understand the core concepts of Disaster Recovery (DR) and High Availability (HR), assess the risks of neglecting these strategies, and discuss the benefits and challenges of implementing them within a business context.

## Content

| Lesson | Est. Delivery Time | Skills |
| ------ |:------------------:| ------ |
| [What are Disaster Recovery (DR) and High Availability (HA)?](./what-are-disaster-recovery-dr-and-high-availability-ha/README.md) | 15 min | Define DR and HA and understand when each is required.                                    |
| [The Risks of Ignoring DR and HA](./the-risks-of-ignoring-dr-and-ha/README.md)                                                    | 15 min | Learn the risks of not having HA and DR implemented.                                      |
| [Implementing DR and HA](./implementing-dr-and-ha/README.md)                                                                      | 15 min | Discuss the key benefits and challenges a business faces by implementing DR and HA plans. |
| **Total content**                                                                                                                 | 45 min |                                                                                           |
